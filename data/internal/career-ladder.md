# Forgeline Engineering Career Ladder (IC track)

*Owner: Arjun Malhotra (CTO) & Zoe Adler (People) · v3, effective 2026-01-15*

Promotions are evaluated twice a year (H1 cycle: June, H2 cycle: December). A promotion case needs evidence of operating **at the next level for ~2 quarters**, written up by the manager with peer input. Calibration is done by all eng managers plus Grace Liu (L6).

Five dimensions: **Scope**, **Technical leadership**, **Impact**, **Collaboration**, **Mentoring**.

---

## L3 — Engineer
- **Scope:** Well-defined tasks and small features within one module. Asks for help early.
- **Technical leadership:** Writes clean, tested code following team conventions. Contributes to code review.
- **Impact:** Ships reliably on sprint commitments.
- **Collaboration:** Communicates status clearly within the team.
- **Mentoring:** Helps onboard interns/new hires on tooling.

## L4 — Senior Engineer
- **Scope:** Owns a significant component or service end-to-end (e.g., a `forgeline-core` module). Breaks ambiguous problems into a plan for their own work.
- **Technical leadership:** Recognized go-to expert for their area. Debugs hard production issues. Writes small design notes for changes inside their component.
- **Impact:** Delivers features that move a team KR.
- **Collaboration:** Works effectively with adjacent teams on interfaces and incidents.
- **Mentoring:** Mentors L3s through code review and pairing.

## L5 — Staff Engineer
- **Scope:** Problems that span **multiple teams or services**. Identifies the problems worth solving, not just the solutions to assigned ones. Comfortable owning something nobody has asked them to own.
- **Technical leadership:** **Sets technical direction through written design docs / RFCs** that are reviewed and adopted beyond their own team. Expected: at least 1–2 substantial, cross-team design docs per half. Makes and documents trade-offs others build on.
- **Impact:** **Measurable business impact** — their work clearly moves a company-level goal (ARR, retention, win rate) or a department KR beyond their own team. Can articulate impact in numbers.
- **Collaboration:** **Leads cross-team initiatives** from proposal through delivery: aligns managers and PMs, negotiates scope, gets the work onto the roadmap. Influences roadmap priorities rather than only executing tickets.
- **Mentoring:** Mentors L3/L4 engineers across teams; grows the next owners of their area so they aren't a single point of failure.

> Common L4→L5 gap (from calibration notes): "Excellent executor and debugger, but impact is bounded by the tickets they're given. Needs to pick a cross-team problem, write the doc, rally people, and ship it."

## L6 — Principal Engineer
- **Scope:** Company-wide technical strategy; multi-quarter bets.
- **Technical leadership:** Defines architecture across the platform; arbiter on contentious technical decisions.
- **Impact:** Work changes the company's competitive position.
- **Collaboration:** Trusted partner to the CTO and exec team; represents engineering with key customers.
- **Mentoring:** Develops Staff engineers; shapes the ladder and hiring bar.

## Evidence examples by level (for promo packets)
| Signal | L4 | L5 |
|---|---|---|
| Design doc | Component-level design note | Cross-team RFC, adopted by 2+ teams |
| Incident | Fixes root cause in own service | Drives systemic fix across services + follow-up program |
| Roadmap | Executes assigned roadmap items | Proposes a new roadmap item and gets it staffed/owned |
| Metrics | Moves team metric (latency, error rate) | Moves company/dept KR (win rate, churn, task success) |
| People | Mentors 1–2 L3s on own team | Mentors across teams; others lead parts of their initiative |
