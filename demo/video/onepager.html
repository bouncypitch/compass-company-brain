The story

Every company already has a brain, scattered across Slack, email, meetings, roadmaps, code and reviews. In the demo, Jordan, a senior engineer at the fictional startup Forgeline, asks Compass: "How do I get promoted to Staff?"

  1. Where you stand: ladder and reviews put Jordan at 40% ready for Staff.
  2. Why now: research agents show 5 of 7 competitors converging on codebase memory.
  3. Your highest-alignment project: R-14 Agent memory. It's unowned, blocks 7 goals and a $610K renewal, and lives in code Jordan owns.
  4. Make it yours: one click claims it, writes the decision into HydraDB and drafts the pull request.
  5. Your 90 days and who to talk to, from the live company graph.

Features

Agentic market radar

Research agents fan out per competitor and intent (launches, hiring and leadership, contracts, papers, acquisitions, pricing). They cover Cursor, Cognition, GitHub Copilot, Replit, OpenAI Codex, Google and Amazon. Every signal is cited, then scored into a capability landscape.

Validated against the future

We hid every signal after Aug 1 and predicted from the earlier data alone. It called Cursor's Rollouts, Google's Antigravity enterprise push, Replit's governance tools and Codex's team workspaces, all shipped after the cutoff.

Promotion coach

Proposes high-impact projects by weighing the market, the engineer's skills, what their manager is rated on, department objectives and the company north star. It looks for the strongest alignment between personal and company goals.

Strategy → pull request

Maps competitor moves onto the roadmap, KRs and code, then acts. Accepting a recommendation writes it back into the brain (so it learns) and drafts a PR against the real codebase.

Ask anything, by voice

Cited answers with the HydraDB graph paths behind them, including today's keynote recording. The exact moment is playable: "…not to just ask questions, but also then to take action."

Company graph

People, teams, KRs, roadmap, code, conversations, customers and tickets, from systems of record. Zoom from the company to a team to a person to a roadmap item.
